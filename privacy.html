Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This site collects information that visitors choose to submit through the Membership, Volunteer, and Complaint forms — including name, phone number, CNIC number, and address. This information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized district administrators. It is used solely to process membership, volunteer coordination, and to follow up on reported issues.

We do not sell or share submitted information with third parties. Data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure with access-controlled security rules.

To request that your information be corrected or removed, use the Contact page.
